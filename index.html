Act I: Chaos → Structure

From noise, signal emerges.

Traditional chat buries your insights in an endless scroll. YMind listens differently—parsing your stream of consciousness into four cognitive primitives in real-time.

◆
Fact
The anchors. What's objectively true.
◆
Friction
The blocks. What's creating resistance.
◆
Spark
The breakthroughs. Aha moments captured.
◆
Action
The commitments. What you'll actually do.
Powered by Gemini Structured Output — no regex hacks, just reliable JSON parsing in real-time.
Act II: Structure → Pattern

The pattern you've been avoiding finally has a name.

One session shows you today. Multiple sessions reveal who you've been becoming. YMind reads your entire history to surface the invisible threads—recurring fears, returning sparks, the same friction wearing different masks.

"Why does your anxiety about coding look exactly like your anxiety about dating?" — Cross-domain pattern
"This spark from January... it became an action in March." — Growth trajectory
Powered by Gemini 3 Pro's 2M context window — reads all your sessions, no lossy summarization. Your entire cognitive history in one analysis.
Act III: Pattern → Action

Insight without execution is just expensive daydreaming.

Awareness alone changes nothing. YMind challenges you to crystallize vague realizations into concrete commitments—exportable cards that survive the session and guide your week.
